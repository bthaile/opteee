# Start with Profit Mechanism, Not Strategy

## Summary

The core principle is to begin with a clearly identified profit mechanism—a market inefficiency or edge—before selecting a trading structure or strategy. Using the volatility risk premium as an example, the video demonstrates how the same underlying idea can produce losses if expressed through the wrong instrument or structure, making mechanism-first thinking essential to avoid costly execution errors.

## Key takeaways

- **Start with mechanism, not strategy** [00:00] — Identify the profit mechanism (e.g., implied volatility is rich relative to realized volatility) before choosing a structure; this prevents selecting the wrong expression for your idea.
- **Structure determines outcome** [00:00] — The same underlying thesis can lose money if expressed through an inappropriate structure (e.g., a vertical spread applied backwards to the market condition).
- **Four-question framework** [00:00] — Use a systematic chain of questions to work from profit mechanism down to the optimal structure that capitalizes on your edge.
- **Volatility risk premium as example** [00:00] — When implied volatility is rich relative to realized volatility, the variance/volatility risk premium is the mechanism; structure selection follows from this observation.

## Notable quotes

> "You can have an idea and then based on your expression you literally could lose money because you use the wrong expression."
