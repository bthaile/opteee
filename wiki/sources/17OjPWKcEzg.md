# How to Know if Your Hedge Is Actually Worth It

## Summary

A framework for evaluating whether a hedge justifies its cost by examining its impact on key portfolio metrics: maximum drawdown reduction, upside sacrifice, worst-year performance, and terminal wealth. The analysis balances protection against opportunity cost across multiple dimensions.

## Key takeaways

- **Drawdown impact** [00:00] — Assess how much your hedge reduces maximum drawdown; this is the primary benefit of hedging.
- **Upside cost** [00:00] — Quantify what percentage of gains you give up during bull markets as the price of protection.
- **Worst-year analysis** [00:00] — Compare your worst single-year return with and without the hedge to understand tail-risk mitigation.
- **Terminal wealth** [00:00] — Calculate ending portfolio value over a full cycle to see net effect of hedge cost versus protection benefit.
- **Multi-metric evaluation** [00:00] — Don't rely on a single metric; hedge quality emerges from the trade-off across drawdown, upside, worst-year, and long-term wealth.
