# GameStop - Ryan Cohen Stock Buying and Options Chain Analysis | Ep64

## Summary

Ryan Cohen and other GameStop insiders have executed significant share accumulation over the past month, with Cohen alone purchasing ~700k shares at $24.41 on the day of recording (~$17M), following a pattern of ~$100M+ in recent purchases. The options market shows elevated call skew (25-delta IV at 68% vs. 46% puts), heavy short call positioning in near-term expirations, and unusual activity in October contracts; GameStop has also been selling covered calls against its Bitcoin holdings via Coinbase Credit at strikes of $105–110k, generating ~$5.8M in premium income over recent quarters.

## Key takeaways

- **Insider accumulation campaign** [00:00–02:00]: RC purchased 700k shares at $24.41 today (~$17M); 450k shares on Sept 29 (~$11M); 1.15M shares on Sept 21 (~$26M); 1M shares on Oct 10 (~$20M). Other officers (Larry Chang, etc.) also accumulating. Pattern suggests coordinated conviction signaling.

- **Earnings surprise: highest profitability on record** [06:12–08:00]: Q2 2025 EPS of $0.27 vs. historical average of $0.09; driven by strong cost-cutting and operational efficiency despite year-over-year sales decline. Collectibles segment scaled to ~$171M in Q4 2025 (up from ~$137M), now ~45% of business.

- **Institutional positioning shift** [17:00–20:29]: Rockefeller Capital, BlackRock (7.3% of outstanding), and other major holders expanding positions. RC's direct purchases dwarf all institutional accumulation combined.

- **Bitcoin covered-call strategy** [24:48–32:19]: GameStop pledged ~4,709 BTC (~$500M at ~$106k/coin) to Coinbase Credit and sold covered calls at strikes $105–110k (maturities through March 2026). Contracts expired worthless ~May 29; position rolled to $80k strike (below cost basis, locking in loss on covered portion). Premium income ~$5.8M/quarter. Eric notes this caps upside if BTC rallies significantly.

- **Options chain: heavy short call positioning** [33:54–37:58]: 30% of volume at ask or above (closing transactions). 16 Oct expiration shows even bullish/bearish premium split but majority in calls (short call dominance). 25-delta call IV 68% vs. 46% puts (4th percentile skew) — reflects GameStop's ability to gap higher on catalysts. Most volume in 2 Oct and 9 Oct (front-month closures); new positioning in 30 Oct (possibly warrant-related); deep chain in 15 Jan.

- **Dark pool activity** [39:52–40:00]: 3.7M shares traded in after-hours on recent days; typical for end-of-day VWAP block trades. No anomalous behavior detected.

- **Warrant expiration** [09:26–13:58]: Warrants expire Oct 30, 2026. IV at ~58 on that date, similar to Oct 23 (no excessive volatility priced in). Cohen retains ability to adjust strike/expiration or issue new warrants. Speculation on capital raise purpose (eBay acquisition thesis, patient capital deployment).
