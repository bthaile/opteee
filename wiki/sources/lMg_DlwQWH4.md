# Does Low GEX Mean Bigger Moves?

## Summary

This analysis examines the relationship between gamma exposure (GEX) and subsequent market volatility. The data shows a monotonic relationship: low-GEX days are followed by median moves of approximately 1.3–1.4%, while high-GEX days see moves roughly half that size. The pattern is robust and viable, though the presenter cautions that other metrics may offer superior predictive power.

## Key takeaways

- **Low GEX → larger follow-on moves**: Median move after low-GEX days is ~1.3–1.4%, compared to ~0.65–0.7% after high-GEX days [00:00]
- **Monotonic relationship**: The gradient is consistent across the GEX spectrum, suggesting a genuine mechanical relationship rather than noise [00:00]
