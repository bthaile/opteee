# Should You Buy or Sell Options?

## Summary

This video explores the fundamental asymmetry between long premium (buying options) and short premium (selling options) strategies. Long premium strategies lose small amounts frequently but occasionally capture large gains; short premium strategies win small amounts consistently but face catastrophic tail risk. The core insight is that neither approach has an inherent edge—success depends on applying filters and discipline to build a genuine trading edge.

## Key takeaways

- **Long premium payoff structure** [00:00]: Frequent small losses punctuated by occasional large wins; requires patience and conviction to endure drawdowns.
- **Short premium payoff structure** [00:00]: Frequent small wins followed by rare but devastating losses that can wipe out accumulated gains; tail risk is the critical management challenge.
- **No inherent edge in either approach** [00:00]: The choice between buying and selling options is not about which is "better"—both require deliberate edge-building through filters and discipline.
- **Strategy selection requires context** [00:00]: Success depends on matching your risk tolerance, capital, and psychological profile to the payoff structure you can sustain.
