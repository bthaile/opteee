# Can You Time Value Stocks?

## Summary

This analysis examines whether value stocks—currently trading at historically cheap valuations relative to growth stocks (87th percentile since 1926)—represent a timeable opportunity. While the statistical signal points in the right direction, the T-statistic of 1.6 suggests moderate conviction rather than overwhelming evidence, explaining approximately 6% of year-to-year performance swings.

## Key takeaways

- **Valuation extremes as context** [00:00]: Value stocks are trading at the 87th percentile of cheapness relative to growth since 1926, indicating an unusually wide valuation gap.
- **Statistical signal strength** [00:00]: The directional signal favors value mean reversion, but the 1.6 T-statistic reflects weak-to-moderate statistical power—not a high-conviction setup.
- **Explanatory power** [00:00]: Value timing explains roughly 6% of year-to-year performance variation, leaving substantial unexplained variance.
- **Trader perspective** [00:00]: From a discretionary trading lens, the setup warrants a lean toward value, but with measured conviction rather than aggressive positioning.
