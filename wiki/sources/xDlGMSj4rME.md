# The GME Gamma Ramp Explained: Can It Actually Happen?

## Summary

Eric analyzes unusual options activity in GameStop (GME) following insider buying by Ryan Cohen, identifying what appears to be a manufactured gamma ramp in the front-month call chain. The video examines elevated call skew, backwardated term structure, and anomalous volume-to-open-interest ratios at near-the-money strikes, explaining the mechanics of how dealer hedging can amplify price moves when multiple call contracts cluster tightly around current price levels.

## Key takeaways

- **Insider buying catalyst** [00:00–00:30]: Ryan Cohen's recent capital deployment into GME signals management alignment; stock up ~17% from the 10th.

- **Extreme call-to-put imbalance** [00:59–01:30]: 1.3M calls vs. 335K puts open interest; 384K call volume vs. 32K put volume today, with calls bought at ask and puts sold at bid—bullish configuration.

- **Risk premium elevated** [01:30–02:00]: Implied volatility ~1 standard deviation above mean (9.8); 30-day IV near 80th percentile, indicating substantial vol priced in.

- **Backwardated term structure** [02:18–02:45]: Front-month (Sept 25) IV at 82% vs. subsequent months at 70%, 68%—signals near-term volatility expectation typical of upside moves.

- **Call skew expansion across curve** [02:45–03:51]: Negative skew (put IV minus call IV) widening; call volatility expanding, not put volatility contracting. Sept 25 calls trading at ~5 standard deviations of premium; Oct 16 calls at ~3 standard deviations—anomalous for 2–3 days to expiration.

- **Gamma ramp signature** [05:08–06:35]: Volume-to-OI ratios at ATM strikes (e.g., 28K volume / 8.5K OI on 24 calls) suggest coordinated buying. As dealers hedge each ITM tranche, they buy underlying, pushing price higher and forcing hedges at the next strike—cascading effect.

- **Skew premium across entire curve** [06:35–07:00]: Not isolated to front month; Oct 16 29-delta calls at 68% IV vs. comparable 27-delta puts at 48% IV shows structural call richness.

- **Mixed intraday flow** [07:53–08:30]: Combination of call buying (speculation on upside) and call selling (capturing rich skew). Net long positioning across multiple strikes.

- **Caution on front-month calls** [08:30–end]: Buying near-dated calls is expensive relative to required underlying move; synthetic long or share purchase preferable if bullish thesis is strong.
