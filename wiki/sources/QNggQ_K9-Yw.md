# Rolling SPY Puts 734 Times

## Summary

A systematic backtest of perpetual put-rolling hedges on SPY over 19.5 years, executing 734 rolls across nine different structural designs. The analysis compares mid-price execution versus offer-side slippage, and examines the trade-off between deductible (strike selection) and holding period (DTE) in managing tail risk and hedging costs.

## Key takeaways

- **Long-term roll count**: 734 rolls over 19.5 years demonstrates the operational intensity of maintaining perpetual hedges [00:00]
- **Nine design variants tested**: Strategy was given multiple favorable structural choices to isolate the cost and efficacy of different put-rolling approaches [00:00]
- **Mid vs. offer pricing**: Backtest shows both mid-price (optimistic) and offer-side execution costs, revealing realistic slippage impact at size [00:00]
- **Deductible concept**: Strike selection framed as a "deductible"—the distance below current price at which protection activates [00:00]
- **DTE and term flexibility**: Analysis compares different holding periods and expiration cycles to balance protection frequency and cost [00:00]
