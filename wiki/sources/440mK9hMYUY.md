# Options Fundamentals Quiz and Prac App | Drive-Thru Trading

## Summary

Eric conducts a live options fundamentals quiz and practical application session with a learner (CG), covering the Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), intrinsic vs. extrinsic value, moneyness, and time decay mechanics. The session includes hands-on analysis of SPY options chains across multiple expirations, with a penalty-based learning framework (push-ups for incorrect answers) and homework assignments on theta analysis and forced-flow edge research.

## Key takeaways

- **Gamma acceleration near expiration** [02:35]: Gamma increases as you approach expiration, particularly for out-of-the-money options; this is a critical concept for long option position management.
- **Theta is highest at-the-money, not at expiration** [04:18]: Theta decay accelerates closer to expiration, but the *percentage* of premium lost per day is highest near expiration while *gross theta* (absolute dollar decay) peaks at-the-money.
- **Gross vs. percentage theta distinction** [09:05]: Gross theta measures absolute dollar decay; percentage theta measures decay as a fraction of remaining premium. Both are essential for understanding long option behavior.
- **Intrinsic value is independent of time** [10:37]: Intrinsic value (how far in-the-money) never changes with time; only extrinsic (time) value decays.
- **Premium = intrinsic + extrinsic** [30:51]: All option value is composed of these two components; understanding their ratio across expirations is foundational.
- **50-delta options can be out-of-the-money** [30:51]: Delta does not equal moneyness; skew and time can create 50-delta OTM options, especially in longer-dated terms.
- **Vega peaks at-the-money and decays with time** [35:12]: Vega is highest for at-the-money options and increases with time to expiration; it tracks extrinsic value concentration.
- **You pay for optionality, not just time** [44:05]: Extrinsic value represents the right to benefit from future price movement, not merely the passage of time.
- **Forced-flow edge hypothesis** [45:58]: Testing whether leveraged ETF rebalancing creates predictable intraday flow patterns on large move days (±3–10%) is a viable edge research direction.
- **Data validation before AI execution** [50:39]: Always verify AI-generated research data and maintain a notebook (Jupyter/Colab) for reproducibility and cross-comparison.

## Notable quotes

- "Extrinsic value is simply time value. That's it. There's nothing to do with the Greeks. Extrinsic value is how much time is associated with that option. Intrinsic value is how far the option is in the money." [30:51]
- "You're not just paying for time. You're paying literally for optionality. The fact that these things can move around and they become more valuable as they move around." [44:05]
