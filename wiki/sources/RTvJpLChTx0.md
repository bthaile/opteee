# GME Just Made New Highs - And Cohen Is Still Buying | Ep65

## Summary

GameStop has cracked new term highs on strong one-sided buy volume, with unprecedented insider buying from the board and Cohen. The 4 DTE options chain shows extreme volatility expansion (call IV at 92%, highest ever recorded), significant gamma ramp positioning at-the-money, and skew premium suggesting market pricing for potential squeeze mechanics. Eric discusses covered strangle mechanics, the fallacy of "missing upside" with covered calls, and models realistic gamma squeeze targets based on current open interest depth.

## Key takeaways

- **New term highs on strong volume** [10:41] — GME cracked above year-long resistance decisively on one-sided buy volume; 9.15M shares traded (~$242M notional), with ~41% attributable to hedging activity at the 30 strike alone.
- **Unprecedented insider buying** [54:24] — Board-wide consistent buying from Cohen and others is historically rare for GME; multiple transactions ongoing, not just isolated sales.
- **Extreme IV expansion across surface** [41:38] — Call IV at 92% (100th percentile, highest ever); put IV also expanding. 25-delta call IV at 92%; total call premium $12.4M (5.5 standard deviations above normal).
- **Gamma ramp structure at-the-money** [45:51] — Volume exceeds open interest at 26.5, 28, 28.5 strikes; half-strikes showing unusual activity. 33,000 OI at 30 strike = 3.3M shares = $99M notional hedging exposure.
- **Skew premium signals squeeze potential** [43:07] — 26-delta calls at 96–99 IV vs. 21-delta puts at ~65 IV; unusual for calls to expand alongside puts. Market pricing for potential gamma squeeze, short squeeze, or Roaring Kitty catalyst.
- **Covered strangle mechanics clarified** [11:45], [01:04:51] — Eric runs 3:1 ratio (long 300 shares, short 1 call at 28 strike, still OTM); never fully cover upside if holding equity; also sell puts to accumulate shares at chosen basis.
- **Covered calls are bullish, not bearish** [20:39] — Risk is to downside; max profit to upside; short call loses extrinsic value as underlying rises, accelerating P&L toward max profit without waiting for theta decay.
- **Roaring Kitty made wealth via options, not buy-and-hold** [24:21] — DFV's wealth came from active options trading and position management, not passive holding; he traded derivatives and unwound positions to compound capital.
- **Gamma squeeze price targets** [01:07:19] — Based on current OI: 27.5–28 high probability; 29–30 moderate probability; 32–35 low probability. Targets shift dynamically as chain rebuilds during move.
- **IV crush risk on directional calls** [33:39] — Bought 19 call for $0.46 on 8 Sept; next day underlying +6.1% but IV dropped 17 points; vega loss (~$0.14) offset directional gain; long premium on expensive IV is a trap.
