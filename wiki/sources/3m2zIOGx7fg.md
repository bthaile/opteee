# How to hedge SPY Without Destroying your Profits

## Summary

This video addresses the tension between portfolio protection and profit erosion when using long puts as hedges. Rather than carrying perpetual put protection (which bleeds P&L through theta decay), the host recommends two alternatives: (1) timing put purchases to specific risk scenarios, or (2) dynamically scaling position size down during market weakness and back up on recovery, thereby locking in profits while avoiding the drag of continuous hedge costs.

## Key takeaways

- **Perpetual put hedges are expensive:** Carrying long puts at all times to cap losses at 10% destroys profitability through continuous theta decay [00:00–00:30].
- **Scenario-based hedging:** Identify specific market conditions where you *would* want protection, then buy puts only around those periods rather than holding them indefinitely [00:30–00:45].
- **Scaling as an alternative to puts:** When the market slows or declines, reduce position size to lock in profits and lower total exposure; if the market recovers, scale back in [00:45–01:00].
- **Avoid the put-decay trap:** The cost of perpetual protection is not worth the peace of mind if it eliminates your edge and returns [01:00–01:15].
- **Risk is your compensation:** Traders are paid to take risk; over-hedging negates that compensation structure [00:15–00:25].
