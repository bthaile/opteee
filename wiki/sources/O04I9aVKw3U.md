# You Can Be 100% Right on Direction and Still Lose Money

## Summary

Directional correctness alone does not guarantee profitability in options trading. The structure chosen to express a directional view—whether near-term or long-dated, premium-buying or premium-selling—creates vastly different sensitivities to gamma, theta, and vega that can overwhelm the underlying directional move. Understanding the trade-offs between these Greeks is essential to aligning your position structure with your thesis and time horizon.

## Key takeaways

- **Direction is necessary but insufficient** [00:00–00:30]: Being right on direction requires you to be right *fast enough* and in the *right structure* to capture that move before time decay or volatility shifts erode your position.

- **Structure determines Greek exposure** [00:30–01:00]: The same bullish thesis can be expressed as a near-term call (high gamma, high theta bleed), a longer-dated call (lower gamma, lower theta), or a call spread (defined risk, different vega profile). Each has radically different P&L curves.

- **Near-term options: gamma and theta dominance** [01:00–01:30]: Buying calls very close to expiration gives you high gamma (convexity and acceleration of gains) but you pay dearly in theta decay. You need the move to happen *immediately* to overcome time bleed.

- **Longer-dated options: lower gamma, lower theta** [01:30–02:00]: Extending duration reduces theta burn and gives you more time for the directional move to play out, but you sacrifice gamma convexity and take on more vega sensitivity to volatility shifts.

- **Vega sensitivity varies by structure** [02:00–02:30]: Near-term options are relatively vega-insensitive; longer-dated options carry significant vega exposure. A volatility crush can hurt a long-dated call even if direction is correct.

- **Trade-off framework** [02:30–03:00]: Choosing structure is about consciously accepting certain Greek exposures while minimizing others. Align your structure to your conviction on timing, not just direction.

## Notable quotes
