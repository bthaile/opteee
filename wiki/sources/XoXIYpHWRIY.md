# GameStop Rally, Ryan Cohen Buys Again, and OPEX | Ep66

## Summary

Ryan Cohen has made two major share purchases in October (844,000 shares for ~$22.8M on the second purchase, plus a $17M position earlier in the month), bringing his total stake to 9.1% and averaging up into strength rather than weakness. With GameStop warrants expiring October 30th at a $32.50 strike, the options market is pricing only a 17% probability of in-the-money expiration, though the timing of Cohen's buying suggests potential price-support intent. The session analyzes warrant pricing via Black-Scholes, compares current squeeze metrics to prior rallies, and examines the elevated call skew and hedging dynamics in the options chain.

## Key takeaways

- **Cohen's buying pattern**: Two purchases in October (17M and 22.8M) at prices of $26–27, averaging up into strength; timing aligns with warrant expiration [14:07–17:06]
- **Warrant probability calculation**: Using Black-Scholes, the $32 strike on Oct 30 warrants implies ~17% probability of finishing in-the-money, with ND2 = 24% and implied volatility ~163% [18:12–26:43]
- **Delta vs. probability distinction**: ND1 (delta approximation) ≈ 35%, but true probability of touch (ND2) ≈ 24%; traders can exploit discrepancies between warrant and call pricing [25:06–28:00]
- **Options chain extremes**: Call-to-put volume ratio reached 455k calls vs. 60k puts on OPEX; 30-day IV expanded from 40s to 74% while realized vol stayed 45–49% [47:11–58:56]
- **Squeeze readiness assessment**: Current metrics show potential but not imminent squeeze; realized vol too low (46% vs. 338% in May 2024), option delta to stock volume at 104% (needs growth), and hedging density concentrated at $30 strike [01:00:18–01:04:38]
- **Key resistance level**: $30 strike shows largest open interest; break above Oct 15 high and $30 would signal increased squeeze potential [01:03:10–01:04:38]
