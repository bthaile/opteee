# The Impact of Sizing in Options Trading

## Summary

This video demonstrates how position sizing dramatically affects strategy performance outcomes, even when the underlying strategy maintains consistent positive edge. Using Monte Carlo simulation across 4,000 paths of a 300-trade derivation, the analysis shows that identical strategies sized at 1×, 2×, and 4× risk levels produce vastly different equity curves and drawdown profiles, illustrating that sizing decisions are as critical as strategy selection itself.

## Key takeaways

- **Same edge, different outcomes**: A strategy with ~3.5% edge per dollar at risk produces radically different performance curves when sized at 1×, 2×, and 4× the base risk level [00:00–00:30]
- **Base sizing context**: The 1× baseline uses 12.5% risk per trade (yellow line), establishing the reference performance [00:15]
- **Leverage amplifies both gains and drawdowns**: The 2× sizing (blue line) and 4× sizing (orange line) show how leverage magnifies volatility and recovery dynamics [00:20–00:40]
- **Sizing is a first-order performance driver**: Position sizing decisions rival strategy selection in importance for long-term outcomes [00:35–00:45]
- **Monte Carlo validation**: Simulating 4,000 paths across 300 trades provides robust evidence that sizing effects are structural, not noise [00:05–00:15]

## Notable quotes
