# NVDA, Anthropic IPO, and Earnings | Trader Hangout

## Summary

Eric hosts a casual trader hangout discussing current market conditions, with focus on Nvidia's recent volatility and earnings setup, GameStop's covered strangle position, and the FAA ground stop incident. The session covers practical options decision-making frameworks, particularly around timing, implied volatility, and vertical spread construction for directional trades.

## Key takeaways

- **Options require specificity on three dimensions** [14:32–16:54]: Time opinion is non-negotiable; you cannot eliminate time decay from options. Implied volatility and direction are secondary and can be isolated, but time cannot. This is the primary filter for whether options are suitable for a trade.

- **Nvidia short-term vs. long-term bias** [30:03–32:44]: Long-term bullish on Nvidia fundamentals; Jensen Huang is not a single point of failure despite recent FUD. Near-term short bias due to elevated volatility and recent selloff. Earnings coming into this volatility environment is unusual and creates interesting pot-odds plays to the upside.

- **Nvidia vertical spread construction** [38:25–40:52]: For a 60-day bullish outlook (e.g., Nvidia above $190), sell the 190 call and buy the 185 call to define risk. Odds are solid (~$1.13 risk for ~$3+ potential return). Can scale wider (175/190) for more premium but higher cost and different risk profile.

- **GME covered strangle update** [21:02–24:40]: Position at 61% utilization with 2,000 shares at $21.02 basis, 8× Jan 18 $24 puts, 14× Jan 18 $30 calls (20 delta, far OTM). Year-to-date ROIC ~38%; underlying down ~10% from campaign start. Implied vol at 91st percentile (one standard deviation up); most premium concentrated in Jan 15 term. Short calls create dampening effect on upside moves.

- **Implied vol vs. realized vol divergence** [22:30–24:40]: GME showing unusual pattern of expanding implied vol day-over-day while realized vol contracts. Call skew present but flattening; 20-delta puts at 78th percentile. Net premium and volume slightly bullish on call side.

- **FAA ground stop and market implications** [43:16–49:05]: Nationwide ground stop lasted ~4 hours; first since 9/11. Caused crypto selloff. Press conferences unclear and unhelpful. Occurred after market close, so no direct equity impact but potential for Sunday futures and follow-on effects.

- **Media bias and negative content consumption** [50:15–51:21]: Negative news dominates social media and content platforms because it drives engagement. Doomberg example: smart business model built on bearish framing. Responsibility lies with consumers to filter and not amplify doom narratives.

- **Earnings volatility term structure** [35:30–37:16]: Nvidia earnings vol is elevated but not extreme; decent amount of vol priced in but not isolated to earnings event. Makes standard short-vol-through-earnings play less attractive. Last 8 earnings had 3 outside moves, mostly trivial.
