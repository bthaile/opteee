# The Two Reasons Value Investing Works

## Summary

Value investing generates returns through two distinct mechanisms: a **risk premium** (compensation for holding riskier assets) and a **behavioral/duration premium** (compensation for holding positions over time as growth compounds). Understanding these profit mechanisms is foundational to evaluating why value strategies work.

## Key takeaways

- **Risk premium mechanism** [00:15] — Value stocks are inherently riskier assets; investors are compensated for bearing that risk over time.
- **Duration/behavioral premium** [00:30] — Investors are paid for the act of holding and waiting; growth compounds over the holding period, rewarding patience.
- **Profit mechanism analysis** [00:45] — When evaluating any strategy, identify *why* it might generate returns; these two factors explain value's edge.
