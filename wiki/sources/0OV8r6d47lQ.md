# One of the Typical GEX Assumptions is Wrong   And So Are Most Others

## Summary

This video challenges a foundational assumption in options market microstructure: that dealers are systematically long call gamma and short put gamma due to customer order flow imbalance. The speaker demonstrates that SPX customer flow is actually balanced across both calls and puts, invalidating this widely-used dashboard convention and highlighting a broader pattern of unexamined assumptions in options analytics.

## Key takeaways

- **Dealer gamma positioning assumption is inverted** [00:00–00:27]: Standard dashboards assume customers sell calls and buy puts, making dealers long call gamma and short put gamma. This assumption is baked into most risk-management systems.
- **SPX customer flow is balanced, not directional** [00:27–00:45]: Empirical data shows customers buy significant SPX call and put volume *and* sell significant volume on both sides—contradicting the unidirectional flow assumption.
- **Series of cascading false assumptions** [00:45]: The gamma positioning error is one of many unexamined conventions in options analytics that propagate downstream into trading decisions and risk models.
