# David Hunter: The Fed Just Hiked Rates: Here's What You Need to Know | The Outlier Podcast

## Summary

The Fed announced a 25 basis point rate hike—the first in three years—which markets had largely priced in. David Hunter discusses the hawkish tone of Powell's press conference, the likelihood of additional hikes, and his broader thesis that this may be a "one-and-done" cycle. Despite near-term headwinds from Iran tensions and oil prices, Hunter remains bullish on equities, targeting S&P 500 at 10,000, and expects a significant rally in precious metals before a longer-term bust driven by leverage and geopolitical risk.

## Key takeaways

### Dated market read (2026-09-16)

- **Fed hike reaction**: Markets initially rallied on the 25 bps hike announcement; weakness came during Powell's press conference when he emphasized price stability and suggested policy is "not restrictive enough" [02:48–03:12]
- **Powell's hawkish tone**: Powell stated inflation is "above target by quite a bit" and the economy is strengthening, not weakening, which surprised some traders expecting a more dovish stance [03:37–04:21]
- **Small-cap underperformance**: Small caps trended notably lower into the FOMC event, a divergence from typical pre-hike patterns [00:02–00:32]
- **Bond market stability**: The 10-year yield remained near 5% post-announcement; the bond market showed "pretty well behaved" response [09:26–09:47]
- **Oil price spike**: Crude rallied from low 80s/high 90s to ~$105 in days due to Iran tensions; Hunter sees this as overdone and expects rollover [23:41–24:00]
- **Mortgage rates elevated**: 30-year mortgages over 7% (cited as 7.17% or higher), pressuring housing demand in Dallas, Atlanta, and southern markets [10:38–10:59]

### Evergreen mechanics

- **One-and-done hike probability**: Hunter argues core inflation is contained (~2.4%), and this may be the only hike in the cycle; traders currently price ~52% probability of a December hike [07:26–09:04, 16:13–16:43]
- **Forward guidance removal**: Powell's decision to abandon forward guidance is correct; markets are better predictors of inflation and rates than Fed committees via the "law of large numbers" [13:31–15:49]
- **Rate peak thesis**: Hunter believes rates are at or near a three-year top (5.01–5.02%), having retested October 2023 highs; expects rates to decline in the "not too distant future" [17:47–18:36]
- **Economic slowdown ahead**: Despite strong earnings, Hunter expects slowing in consumer and housing sectors due to higher rates; manufacturing and AI remain strong [10:11–10:38]
- **Sentiment as contrarian indicator**: Wall Street is nervous and "one foot out the door"; this skepticism suggests a top is not imminent—tops form when sentiment is complacent [37:55–38:59]
- **AI infrastructure as multi-year theme**: Data center construction, power needs, and commodity demand for AI buildout will persist for years, even if AI stocks correct sharply [41:06–42:02]

## Notable quotes
