# When the Wheel Strategy ACTUALLY Beats SPY

## Summary

The wheel strategy—a systematic approach of selling cash-secured puts and covered calls—outperforms the S&P 500 during specific market regimes: sideways/consolidation periods (2011, 2015) and bear markets (2008, 2022). The premium collection acts as a dampening effect on drawdowns while capturing income during low-volatility environments.

## Key takeaways

- **Sideways markets favor the wheel** [00:00]: In 2011 and 2015, when the market moved sideways with low directional conviction, the wheel strategy outperformed SPY due to consistent premium collection.
- **Premium acts as a cushion in bear markets** [00:00]: During 2008 and 2022 downturns, the wheel's income generation dampened losses relative to buy-and-hold SPY.
- **Regime-dependent outperformance**: The wheel's edge is strongest when volatility is elevated (bear markets) or when price action is range-bound (sideways consolidation), not during strong bull runs.
