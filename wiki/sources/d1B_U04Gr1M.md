# Secret Spies Tell No Lies - NVDA and Jensen | Stock Market Analysis

## Summary

Eric discusses the market implications of recent news regarding Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's alleged extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese intelligence operative. The stream focuses on Nvidia's elevated implied volatility (98th percentile into earnings), term structure dynamics, and trading opportunities across the volatility curve. Despite leadership uncertainty, the market is pricing significant upside skew, suggesting either confidence in Huang's retention or indifference to his potential removal.

## Key takeaways

- **Leadership scandal impact** [20:14]–[23:36]: Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang reportedly had affairs with an employee and a woman believed by US intelligence to be a Chinese spy; board preparing to meet as soon as Wednesday to determine his future.

- **Volatility structure** [21:54]–[26:28]: Implied volatility at 98th percentile into earnings (Nov 17); near-term vol (5-day) at 10th percentile, 2-week at median, 60-day at ~75th percentile; negative near-term VRP with longer-term vol elevated.

- **Put-call skew** [28:08]–[29:52]: 157.5 puts (2.5 OTM, 0.2 delta) offer minimal premium; 180 calls (1 SD out) offer ~$1 per $5 spread; entire curve shows upside skew, indicating market pricing a floor.

- **Supplier vs. hyperscaler divergence** [32:10]: Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta) holding up better; semiconductor suppliers down significantly from June peak, more sensitive to Nvidia uncertainty.

- **Trading setup** [33:53]–[35:14]: Ratio call diagonal (short near-term, long dated) or long shares viable; 365-day vol at 50th percentile; June 2027 calls at 42% vol (40th percentile), December 2027 at 52nd percentile—no bargains, but reasonable for longer-dated exposure.

- **Market positioning** [37:18]–[39:30]: Risk reversal (25-delta) heavily skewed to upside; market not pricing downside heavily; Eric estimates ~70/30 odds Huang is removed, but market behavior suggests either confidence in retention or indifference to outcome.
