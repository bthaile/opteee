# Tom Sosnoff Reacts to His Own Google Search Results and his Long SPCX Trade | Unhedged

## Summary

Tom Sosnoff reviews his Google search autocomplete results, discussing his net worth, family, and career trajectory, then pivots to market analysis. He details a contrarian long position in SpaceX (built ~7–8 days prior at ~$125 strike, now trading ~$110), explains volatility pricing mechanics for recent IPOs, and shares bearish views on semis (especially Micron), cautious positioning in small caps, and tactical long exposure to gold, silver, and bonds as oversold contrarian plays.

## Key takeaways

### Dated market read (2026-07-29)

- **SpaceX bottomed today** [23:03–24:07]: Stock down $6 at open, closed up ~$3 after $9 reversal; Tom believes sellers exhausted. IPO price was ~$160–165; stock traded down to $106–107 this morning. Tom is now long SpaceX, short 90/95 puts (average entry ~$90–95 strike), with position built over past week.
- **Volatility in SpaceX still elevated** [25:05–26:12]: Current IV ~106%, which Tom views as unsustainable for a $100+ stock; expects vol to contract to 60s–80s range. No earnings impact expected; this is a short-premium play.
- **NASDAQ correction underway** [39:00–40:09]: Down ~1,000 points from highs (~31k to ~28k), ~10% drawdown. Tom pausing shorts here but won't go long NASDAQ soon.
- **Fed decision tomorrow: 70% no change, 30% hike** [40:09–41:15]: 10-year bond rally (ZN to 108, now 110.26) has taken pressure off Powell. If bonds drop another point, hike becomes likely.
- **Oil crashed 93→79 in 3 days** [41:15–42:19]: Tom got short at 80 breakout, scratched position. At 79, oil is fairly priced; prefers short premium at 1.5–2× expected move.
- **Gold/silver as contrarian longs** [42:19–44:34]: Gold at 4,000 interesting if it breaks below; silver at 57 level attractive. Gold–silver ratio traded 45–120 in 2026 alone (vs. theoretical 2:1 futures ratio); extremely volatile, untradable as pairs trade.
- **Micron worst trade of year** [37:49–38:56]: Shorted at 600, stock went to 1,250; now at 820, Tom flat. Semis (AMD, Intel, Micron) have rich volatility; Nvidia less so.
- **VIX futures flat despite market stress** [45:43–47:55]: Even with NASDAQ down 500, S&P down 30, VIX futures up only 1%. No shock premium; market structure (US liquidity concentration in top 20–50 stocks) providing buffer.

### Evergreen mechanics

- **Tom's core strategy** [18:08–19:20]: Active contrarian, premium seller. Indifferent to product/strategy. Makes ~15,000–16,000 trades/year (~64 trades today, "average to slow day"). Junkie for clicking the button.
- **Volatility pricing for new IPOs** [32:31–35:38]: Market makers (lead firm ~90% Citadel) price IV initially, then adjust based on order flow. SpaceX IV priced mid-90s initially, now low 100s. Normalization typically 15 hours to 48 hours max; used to take 1–3 days pre-software sophistication.
- **Term structure expectations** [35:38–36:43]: SpaceX Feb 2027 IV at 84%; Dec 2027 at 74%. Tom expects these to compress; stock will eventually price like Tesla, not Rocket Lab.
- **Sector rotation and breadth divergence** [30:37–31:30]: Daily/bidaily rotation between chip stocks and software; money flowing in/out. Google spending $45B capex vs. $5–6B free cash flow loss; Tom unsure if cyclical or accounting. Companies "too big to fail" with ample cash.
- **Bond-rate feedback loop** [40:09–41:15]: 10-year at 110.26 = "no-brainer do nothing" for Fed. At 111, definitely no hike. Below 110, hike likely. Powell "doesn't want to raise" but may be forced.
- **Liquidity concentration risk** [47:55–48:52]: US market structure concentrates liquidity in top 20–50 stocks/assets, creating "almost unbreakable" stabilizing buffer. Rest of world hasn't caught up; this is why broad VIX stays muted despite individual stock volatility.
