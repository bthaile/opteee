# The Resilience of Value Investing

## Summary

An analysis of value investing's historical performance and recent underperformance. The discussion examines whether value's century-long outperformance over expensive stocks remains valid despite significant drawdowns in recent years, with particular focus on the 2000–2020 period and the structural resilience of the value premium.

## Key takeaways

- **Long-term validity of value premium**: Over a century, cheap stocks have objectively beaten expensive stocks, establishing value as a legitimate risk premium [00:00]
- **Volatility within the premium**: Value lost money in approximately 42% of years historically, with a best year of +45% (2000) and worst year of −31% (2020), demonstrating that periodic losses do not invalidate the long-term edge [00:00]
- **Recency bias risk**: Recent underperformance should not be conflated with structural breakdown; the question remains whether the value premium persists going forward [00:00]
