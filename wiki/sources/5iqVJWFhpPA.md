# 0DTE Options Greeks Explained | Stop Making This Mistake

## Summary

This video dissects how the Greeks behave in zero days-to-expiration (0DTE) options, using live SPX market data to illustrate the amplified volatility and rapid decay characteristic of this instrument class. The core message: 0DTE trading requires speed and pragmatism; long premium faces decay and timing risk, while short premium wins frequently but faces catastrophic tail losses that can wipe out months of gains in a single day.

## Key takeaways

### Dated market read (2026-07-19)

- **0DTE volume explosion:** Since launch in 2022, 0DTE SPX options now dominate volume by DTE, creating massive infrastructure but also amplifying trader mistakes [00:47].
- **Live example (SPX 7550 call, 16 July):** Entry at ~1.70, immediate drop to ~0.10 within 10 minutes, rally to ~2.45, then slow decay to zero—the typical 0DTE path [01:48].

### Evergreen mechanics

- **Greeks amplify near expiration:** Gamma, theta, delta, and vega all become "jacked up" as you approach 0DTE and at-the-money strikes; gamma peaks at ATM 0DTE, making delta swings violent (halving or doubling in 10–15 minutes) [03:09], [04:08].
- **Gamma peaking is the core problem:** Gamma tells you the rate of change of delta; even delta-hedging becomes nearly impossible because gamma swings flip your position structure entirely within an hour [05:08].
- **Charm (delta decay) works against OTM, for ITM:** If you're out-of-the-money, charm erodes your position; if in-the-money, charm helps [05:38].
- **Market microstructure dominates:** Small spot moves flip gamma all over the place; you're either right or wrong very fast, and outcome depends heavily on path of underlying—forces you cannot predict or control [06:10], [06:38].
- **Long 0DTE options: cheap but deadly:** Options 0.5–1.5% OTM look cheap (10 cents) but 90% expire worthless; 94% of cheap OTM options expired worthless YTD 2026 [07:52], [08:18]. Winners (500%+ to 2,700% returns) do not compensate for the losses; long 0DTE is a losing position on average [09:21].
- **Short 0DTE options: frequent wins, catastrophic tails:** Selling straddles wins 64% of days YTD, but worst day = 20× average daily gain—one loss wipes out an entire month of profit [09:54]. The game is to cut tail risk.
- **Speed is mandatory:** You are "playing hot potato" with a super-hot potato; you must move quickly, especially if long premium [02:47].
