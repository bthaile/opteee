# Why Preparing for a Market Crash Is Killing Your Returns

## Summary

This video challenges the common practice of sizing option positions defensively around worst-case crash scenarios. The core argument is that over-hedging for tail risk prevents traders from deploying meaningful capital and generating returns. Instead, traders should develop a probabilistic understanding of their specific strategy's behavior and size accordingly.

## Key takeaways

- **Avoid crash-scenario sizing** [00:00] — Basing all position sizing on worst-case crash potentials leaves capital idle and prevents profitable risk-taking.
- **Strategy-specific risk assessment** [00:00] — Risk management must be tailored to how your particular strategy actually behaves, not generic market catastrophe assumptions.
- **Probabilistic thinking required** [00:00] — Traders need a clear probabilistic understanding of what their positions are likely to do, not just what *could* happen in extremes.
- **Capital deployment vs. protection trade-off** [00:00] — Over-preparation for crashes creates a false choice: you either take no risk or accept unmanaged risk. The middle ground is disciplined position sizing based on realistic probabilities.
