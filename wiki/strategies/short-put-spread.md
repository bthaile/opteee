# Short Put Spread

A short put spread is a defined-risk vertical spread strategy in which a trader sells a put option at one strike and buys a put option at a lower strike, creating a directional bearish bet with capped maximum loss and profit. The strategy is fundamentally a directional play rather than a volatility trade, requiring a strong thesis about price direction rather than reliance on probability metrics alone; success depends on aligning the position's time frame with the trader's conviction and understanding that the defined risk structure carries inherent negative expected value due to transaction costs. Effective deployment requires first identifying a reliable market pattern or profit mechanism to exploit, then using the short put spread as the vehicle to extract that edge, rather than selecting the structure arbitrarily based on win-rate statistics or technical indicators.
