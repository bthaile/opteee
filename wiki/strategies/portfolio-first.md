# Portfolio First

Portfolio First" is an options-trading approach that prioritizes managing overall portfolio risk and position sizing discipline before entering individual trades, rather than sizing positions based on conviction alone. The strategy employs volatility-normalized position-sizing methods—such as scaling capital inversely to each asset's volatility or using fractional Kelly Criterion formulas—to ensure that each trade contributes proportionally to total portfolio risk and drawdown exposure. By maintaining consistent position-sizing rules and avoiding oversized bets on high-conviction plays, traders can sustain their accounts through inevitable losing periods and compound wealth more reliably over time.
