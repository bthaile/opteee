# Selling Premium

Selling premium is a short options strategy that generates consistent small profits from time decay and volatility compression, but carries asymmetric tail risk where occasional large losses can overwhelm many winning trades. The strategy works by collecting option prices upfront—such as through covered strangles that sell both calls and puts—and relies on the underlying asset staying within a profitable range until expiration. Success requires disciplined filters and positioning analysis, such as reading volatility surfaces and skew patterns to identify when options are expensive relative to realized volatility, rather than selling indiscriminately into all market conditions.
