# Risk Budgeting

Risk budgeting is the practice of allocating capital and leverage across a portfolio based on mathematical frameworks like the Kelly Criterion to manage the probability of catastrophic loss. The approach recognizes that even analytically sound trading strategies can face ruin when leverage and concentration combine without proper position sizing discipline, as illustrated by cases where high conviction theses amplify drawdown risk. Effective risk budgeting requires using fractional Kelly sizing rather than full Kelly to account for real-world uncertainty in edge estimation and the hidden tail risks that standard volatility measures may obscure, particularly in premium-selling strategies.
