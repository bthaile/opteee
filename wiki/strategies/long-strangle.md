# Long Strangle

A long strangle is a multi-leg options structure that combines a long out-of-the-money call and a long out-of-the-money put on the same underlying, typically used to profit from large price moves in either direction when implied volatility is expected to expand. It is employed as a tool to implement specific profit mechanisms—such as capturing implied volatility expansion around earnings or exploiting variance risk premium opportunities—rather than as a standalone edge. The strategy's profitability depends on strike selection, position sizing, and clearly defined entry and exit rules aligned with the trader's volatility outlook and risk tolerance.
