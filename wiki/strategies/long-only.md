# Long Only

Long Only is a directional trading approach where a trader maintains positions exclusively in bullish assets without using short selling or hedging strategies. The strategy's performance depends heavily on market regime and volatility conditions, as calm volatility environments do not guarantee superior returns and offer no inherent protection against sudden repricing events. Successful Long Only implementation requires systematic research, robust backtesting that accounts for transaction costs and drawdown management, and the discipline to follow a predetermined strategy through varying market conditions rather than over-optimizing based on historical results.
