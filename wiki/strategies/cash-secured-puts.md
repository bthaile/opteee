# Cash Secured Puts

A cash-secured put is a short put position backed by reserved cash equal to the strike price, ensuring the seller can purchase the underlying stock if assigned. In the wheel strategy, cash-secured puts form the entry leg, but this approach caps upside profit while retaining full downside risk, creating an asymmetric payoff that works against upward-drifting assets like SPY. The strategy's performance can be improved through selective entry timing based on volatility and moving-average conditions rather than mechanical execution, though even optimized versions tend to underperform buy-and-hold due to the structural limitation of capped gains on both put and call legs.
