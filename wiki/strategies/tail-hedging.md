# Tail Hedging

Tail hedging is a defensive options strategy that uses puts or other protective instruments to guard against severe portfolio drawdowns during market dislocations. The approach requires balancing the cost of protection against its benefit in reducing maximum drawdown and worst-case performance, while accepting some sacrifice of upside returns and terminal wealth. Practitioners employ systematic frameworks to evaluate whether a hedge's insurance value justifies its premium drain, and may adjust or roll positions dynamically as market conditions and portfolio risk evolve.
