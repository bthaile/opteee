# Long Call Spread

A long call spread is a vertical spread strategy in which a trader buys a call option at a lower strike price and sells a call option at a higher strike price, creating a defined-risk, capital-efficient directional bet on upward price movement. The strategy is fundamentally a bullish directional play rather than a volatility trade, with maximum profit capped at the difference between the two strikes minus the net premium paid. Success with long call spreads requires a strong directional thesis and careful position management aligned to the trader's time frame, as the strategy carries inherent negative expected value due to its structure and transaction costs.
