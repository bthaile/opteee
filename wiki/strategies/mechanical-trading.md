# Mechanical Trading

Mechanical trading refers to the use of systematic, rule-based strategies and automated systems (often bot-driven) to execute options trades rather than relying on discretionary decision-making. The approach emphasizes maintaining portfolio neutrality, strict position sizing, and awareness of trading friction costs as the primary levers for consistent profitability over time. Practitioners transition from intuitive "hunter-gatherer" trading to predetermined mechanical frameworks that remove emotional bias and enforce disciplined trade selection, with the goal of achieving repeatable, inflation-beating returns through simplicity and systematic execution.
