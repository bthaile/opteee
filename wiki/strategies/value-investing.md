# Value Investing

Value investing is the strategy of buying stocks trading at low valuations relative to their fundamentals, historically grounded in financial statement analysis and competitive advantage identification. While the approach outperformed markets for roughly eighty years, research shows it has underperformed significantly since 2007, with the valuation gap between value and growth stocks narrowing considerably from historical levels. Modern practitioners have adapted the strategy by combining value with momentum factors or using options-based hedging to improve conditional returns, and some institutional managers emphasize that trend-following and risk management often matter more than pure valuation metrics for long-term success.
