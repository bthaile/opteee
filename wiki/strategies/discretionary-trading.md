# Discretionary Trading

Discretionary trading is an active trading approach where traders make real-time decisions based on market analysis and identified opportunities rather than following mechanical rules, requiring the trader to possess repeatable edge, disciplined position sizing, and psychological resilience to survive long enough to collect profits across sufficient sample sizes. Success in discretionary trading depends less on semantic definitions of skill versus gambling and more on whether a trader can identify exploitable patterns, manage risk effectively, and maintain the specific personality traits and commitment to deliberate practice necessary for consistent performance. While research suggests most retail traders fail at active trading, those who combine discretionary trading with tools for systematic research and signal generation—such as AI-powered analysis platforms—and maintain financial stability through other income sources may improve their odds of viability.
