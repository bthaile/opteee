# Collar

A collar is an options strategy that combines a long stock or underlying position with a protective put (downside hedge) funded by selling an out-of-the-money call, creating a defined risk-reward profile at initiation. Traders use collars to manage conviction and risk by capping both downside loss and upside gain, though the strategy requires active management as the underlying price moves and position deltas shift, forcing traders to reconcile their original thesis with real-time market conditions. The collar exemplifies the broader hedging principle that protection carries a cost—either direct (premium paid) or indirect (opportunity cost from the sold call)—and that effective risk management demands ongoing monitoring rather than passive hold-to-expiration execution.
