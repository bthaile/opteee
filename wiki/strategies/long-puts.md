# Long Puts

Long puts are options contracts purchased to provide downside protection or hedge against portfolio losses, allowing the buyer to profit if an underlying asset declines in value. Backtesting over nearly two decades reveals that the cost of maintaining continuous put hedging is substantial, typically ranging from 2.9–5.1% annually after accounting for premiums paid, rolling costs, and put skew, which significantly erodes returns even when protection occasionally proves valuable during market crises. Rather than perpetual long-put hedging, traders may consider alternatives such as scenario-based hedging, dynamic scaling strategies that adjust protection based on market conditions, or diversified strategy construction to manage downside risk more cost-effectively.
