# Risk Management

Risk management in options trading centers on controlling capital allocation and distinguishing between structural risk (maximum possible loss) and managed risk (actual loss enforced through active management and exit rules). Traders establish portfolio-level drawdown budgets with circuit breakers to trigger de-risking when thresholds are breached, and carefully size positions based on their specific strategy's behavior rather than worst-case scenarios, recognizing that position sizing is as critical as strategy selection itself. Key challenges include accounting for market gaps that can prevent intended exits and the inherent trade-off between hedging costs and upside capture, requiring traders to balance capital preservation with meaningful return generation through sound management plans aligned with their strategy.
