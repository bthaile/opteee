# Volatility Trading

Volatility trading is a strategy centered on capturing the volatility risk premium (VRP)—the persistent gap between implied volatility priced into options and the volatility that actually occurs in the market. Rather than simply selling options when implied volatility appears "high," effective volatility trading requires understanding the interplay between theta decay, gamma losses, and regime dynamics, recognizing that VRP represents genuine compensation for tail risk exposure rather than a free edge. Traders employ various approaches including covered strangles and speculative volatility positions, with success depending on proper position sizing, skill maturation, and the ability to navigate changing market regimes rather than mechanical entry signals.
