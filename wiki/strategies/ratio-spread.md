# Ratio Spread

A ratio spread is an options strategy in which a trader sells a greater number of contracts at one strike price while buying a smaller number at another strike, creating an asymmetric payoff structure that profits from mean reversion or specific directional moves within a defined range. The strategy is employed as part of a diversified multi-strategy portfolio to generate consistent returns across different market regimes, though it requires careful risk management and position sizing to avoid unlimited loss exposure on the short side. Traders using ratio spreads typically combine them with other strategies—such as covered strangles, volatility plays, and directional positions—rather than relying on any single structure, and success depends on proper entry selection based on price levels and portfolio-level risk allocation.
