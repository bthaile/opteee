# Long Put Spread

A long put spread is a vertical spread strategy constructed by buying a put at one strike and selling a put at a lower strike, creating a defined-risk, capital-efficient directional bet on downward price movement. The strategy's profitability depends critically on strike selection, expiration timing, and the trader's directional conviction rather than volatility assumptions alone. Success requires alignment between the position's Greek profile and the trader's thesis duration, as narrow spreads demand extended holding periods for minimal returns while wider structures may better suit stronger directional views. Like all vertical spreads, the strategy carries inherent negative expected value due to its structure and transaction costs, making a robust directional thesis essential rather than relying on probability-based entry signals.
