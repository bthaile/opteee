# Rebalancing

Rebalancing is the practice of adjusting a position's hedge ratio or portfolio composition in response to price movements and changing delta exposure, with frequency directly affecting both profit-and-loss volatility and transaction costs. In options trading, rebalancing mechanics appear across strategies ranging from delta hedging (where frequent rebalancing reduces P&L swings but exhibits diminishing returns) to short-volatility positions like strangles (where underlying moves force delta shifts requiring tactical adjustments) to sector-rotation strategies (where portfolio turnover incurs friction costs that must be weighed against capture ratios). The optimal rebalancing frequency depends on an individual trader's edge, cost structure, and market regime rather than fixed time intervals, and over-rebalancing can erode returns through accumulated transaction costs without proportional risk reduction.
