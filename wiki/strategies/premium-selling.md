# Premium Selling

Premium selling is an options strategy that capitalizes on the volatility risk premium—the tendency for implied volatility priced into options to exceed the volatility that actually occurs—by selling options and collecting the difference as profit through theta decay. Successful premium selling requires understanding that this edge compensates sellers for tail risk exposure and involves managing gamma losses, monitoring regime changes, and sizing positions appropriately rather than simply selling when implied volatility appears elevated. The strategy can be implemented through structures like covered strangles, which balance income generation with portfolio protection, though traders must avoid common pitfalls such as poor backtesting methodology and failure to account for the interplay between time decay and adverse price moves.
