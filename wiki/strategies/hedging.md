# Hedging

Hedging in options trading involves using derivative instruments—such as futures or protective options positions—to offset or reduce risk in an existing portfolio. The channel discusses hedging as a practical application of futures contracts for managing overnight and weekend market moves, as well as through structured positions like covered strangles that provide modest but steady returns as a core portfolio allocation. Hedging strategies involve trade-offs between the cost of protection and expected returns, with consideration given to implied volatility levels and the asymmetric risk profiles of positions like short strangles.
