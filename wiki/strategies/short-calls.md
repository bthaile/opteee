# Short Calls

A short call is an options strategy where a trader sells a call contract, profiting if the underlying asset stays below the strike price or declines, with the seller collecting the premium upfront but facing unlimited loss risk if the price rises sharply. Short calls are used both as standalone bearish or neutral bets and as components of broader strategies like synthetic short positions, where they help establish directional exposure or generate income. The strategy's profitability depends on factors including implied volatility levels, the term structure of options, and dealer hedging dynamics that can amplify price moves when call positions cluster densely around current price levels.
