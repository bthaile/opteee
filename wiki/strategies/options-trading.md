# Options Trading

Options trading involves buying and selling contracts that give the right to buy or sell an underlying asset at a specified price and time, with profitability determined by understanding the Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), intrinsic versus extrinsic value, and time decay mechanics. Traders analyze options chains across multiple expirations to identify positioning, capital mechanics, and edge opportunities, often using objective data analysis rather than pattern-seeking. The field encompasses both standard listed options and customizable FLEX options, which allow specification of strike, expiration, and exercise style while providing exchange clearing to eliminate counterparty risk, though at the cost of reduced liquidity compared to standard contracts.
