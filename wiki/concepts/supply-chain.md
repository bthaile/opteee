# Supply Chain

Supply chain refers to the interconnected systems of manufacturing, logistics, and resource distribution that underpin global markets and economies. In options trading, supply chain dynamics are analyzed as drivers of sector-specific and macro volatility—particularly in energy, semiconductors, defense, and infrastructure—where bottlenecks, geopolitical disruptions, or onshoring policies create pricing inefficiencies and trade opportunities. Traders monitor supply-side constraints (such as transformer lead times, grid capacity, or critical material dependencies) and geopolitical risks to the flow of goods and energy as signals for directional moves and volatility expansion in related equities and derivatives. The fragility of global supply chains, especially Western dependence on foreign manufacturing and energy chokepoints, is treated as a source of tail risk that may not be fully priced into options markets.
