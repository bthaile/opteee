# Pairs Trade

A pairs trade in options involves taking offsetting positions in two related securities—typically a long position in one and a short position in another—to profit from relative mispricings or sector dislocations rather than directional market moves. The strategy is used to isolate specific trading opportunities created by policy announcements or market events, such as exploiting weakness in materials and tech relative to broader indices during tariff uncertainty, or positioning long in one name while short in a correlated peer. By focusing on the spread between two instruments rather than absolute price direction, pairs trades allow traders to reduce systematic market risk and concentrate on the relative value thesis driving the trade.
