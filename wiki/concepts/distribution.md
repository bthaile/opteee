# Distribution

Distribution refers to the process of understanding how a specific profit mechanism behaves across different market conditions—measuring its shape, horizon, half-life, range, frequency, severity, and robustness before constructing a trade around it. Rather than selecting an options structure based on Greek preferences or time decay alone, traders profile how their edge actually manifests in the market, then choose the structure that best captures that specific mechanism's characteristics. This systematic approach prevents overfitting to best-case scenarios and ensures that the selected trade expression (call spread, strangle, etc.) aligns with how the underlying profit opportunity actually distributes across price, time, and volatility.
