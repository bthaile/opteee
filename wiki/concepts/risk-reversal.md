# Risk Reversal

A risk reversal is an options strategy that pairs a long call and short put (or vice versa) to express a directional market view while offsetting the cost of the directional leg through premium collection. The strategy is used to establish bullish or bearish positions with reduced or zero net cost by selling premium in one direction to finance the purchase of protection or upside exposure in the opposite direction. Risk reversals allow traders to align their portfolio positioning with specific market views while managing the capital and margin requirements of outright directional bets.
