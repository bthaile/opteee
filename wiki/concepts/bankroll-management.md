# Bankroll Management

Bankroll management in options trading refers to the systematic sizing and allocation of capital across positions to preserve trading capital and optimize long-term wealth compounding while accounting for strategy edge and risk tolerance. The practice encompasses position sizing frameworks like the Kelly Criterion and fractional Kelly approaches, which mathematically determine optimal bet sizes based on edge and volatility, though real-world application requires conservative adjustments to account for estimation uncertainty and tail risks that standard models underestimate. Poor bankroll management—including martingale-style position doubling, oversizing relative to edge, and emotional deviation from sizing rules—has historically led to catastrophic losses even in strategies with positive expected value, making disciplined sizing as critical to trading success as strategy selection itself.
