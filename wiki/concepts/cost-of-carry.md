# Cost Of Carry

Cost of carry refers to the financing and holding costs embedded in option prices, including interest rates and the expense of maintaining a hedged position over time. In options trading, cost of carry affects how options are priced relative to their underlying asset and becomes a critical drag on strategy returns when positions are held or rolled repeatedly, as demonstrated in long-term hedging backtests where perpetual put protection costs 2.9–5.1% annually net of payouts. Traders must account for cost of carry alongside other friction costs—commissions, slippage, bid-ask spreads, and skew—when evaluating whether a strategy's returns justify its real-world execution expenses.
