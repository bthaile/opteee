# Margin Requirement

Margin requirement is the amount of capital a broker requires a trader to hold in reserve to open and maintain a short options position, serving as a collateral buffer against potential losses. In the context of short puts and other short premium strategies, margin requirement functions as the denominator in risk-adjusted return calculations, allowing traders to compare opportunities by dividing premium received by the margin tied up to produce a return-on-invested-capital metric. Understanding margin requirements is essential for position sizing and evaluating whether the credit received justifies the capital commitment relative to the underlying risk.
