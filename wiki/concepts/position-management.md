# Position Management

Position management in options trading encompasses the active monitoring and adjustment of open positions as market conditions and underlying prices evolve, requiring traders to reconcile their initial thesis with real-time position Greeks and path dynamics rather than passively holding to expiration. Key techniques include delta hedging and gamma scalping to control volatility exposure, with hedging frequency representing a trade-off between reducing P&L volatility and incurring transaction costs—optimal frequency depends on individual edge and cost structure rather than fixed time intervals. Effective position management also requires honest assessment of whether a position's current delta and risk-reward still align with conviction, particularly in strategies like collars where attractive initial risk-reward can deteriorate as the underlying moves. The discipline extends to recognizing that premium collected in short-option positions remains at risk until expiration and can evaporate quickly, making position sizing and tail-risk awareness critical to avoiding catastrophic losses that erase months of gains.
