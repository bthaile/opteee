# Stress Testing

Stress testing in options trading is the process of evaluating how a position or portfolio performs under adverse market conditions to ensure maximum loss and risk exposure remain within acceptable limits before entry. It involves calculating defined risk across different option structures (single options, spreads, iron condors) and simulating positions against historical or hypothetical scenarios to validate that win rates and position sizing align with overall account management goals. Stress testing serves as a critical validation mechanism within the broader research and portfolio construction process, helping traders distinguish between emotionally appealing ideas and those with genuine, tested profit mechanisms that can withstand market regime changes.
