# Sortino Ratio

The Sortino ratio is a statistical metric used to evaluate trading strategy performance by measuring risk-adjusted returns while accounting specifically for downside volatility rather than total volatility. It appears in trade-log templates as a key quantitative measure alongside other metrics like expected return, standard deviation, and maximum drawdown to help traders honestly assess whether their strategy generates genuine edge. The metric is particularly useful for distinguishing between strategies that achieve high returns through skill versus those that simply benefit from favorable market conditions or excessive risk-taking.
