# Protective Puts

Protective puts are long put options purchased to hedge downside risk in equity holdings, providing insurance against sharp market declines. While backtests demonstrate that puts can offer meaningful protection during severe drawdowns and crises, the persistent cost of premiums, rolling expenses, and theta decay erodes returns substantially over longer periods, often outweighing the protection benefit. Rather than maintaining continuous put hedges, traders may achieve better risk-adjusted outcomes through scenario-based hedging (purchasing puts only during elevated risk periods), dynamic position scaling, or diversified strategy construction that avoids the perpetual drag of insurance costs.
