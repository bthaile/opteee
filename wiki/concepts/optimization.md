# Optimization

Optimization in options trading refers to the disciplined process of refining strategy parameters and decision-making based on quantifiable performance data rather than intuition or secondary factors. The core principle is to prioritize the primary profit driver—directional accuracy—before layering in secondary considerations like volatility or hedge ratios, and to use metrics like expected return (expectancy) rather than win rate alone to evaluate true long-term profitability. Effective optimization requires structured documentation through trading plans, strategy outlines, and trade logs that enable objective measurement and iteration, allowing traders to distinguish skill maturation from favorable market conditions and to systematically improve trade selection over time.
