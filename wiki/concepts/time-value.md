# Time Value

Time value, also called extrinsic value, is the portion of an option's premium that exists beyond its intrinsic value and decays over time through theta, the Greeks component measuring daily erosion. As expiration approaches, time decay accelerates, particularly for out-of-the-money options, requiring increasingly larger underlying price moves to recover losses and making cheap OTM options poor risk-reward propositions for most traders. Traders manage time value strategically—some exit positions to preserve remaining time value before expiration, while others exploit time decay through short premium strategies like covered strangles or by managing long-dated positions based on decay mechanics rather than profit targets.
