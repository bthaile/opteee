# Rebalancing

Rebalancing in options trading refers to the active adjustment of positions to maintain a target delta range or risk profile rather than holding passively to expiration or max profit. Common rebalancing approaches include delta bands (which trigger adjustments when delta drifts beyond a defined threshold), gamma scalping (which monetizes realized price moves through frequent hedging), and tolerance-based frameworks that balance the cost of hedging against the benefit of isolating specific risks. The frequency and method of rebalancing directly affect P&L volatility, upside and downside capture, and overall strategy returns, with optimal rebalancing depending on individual edge, cost structure, and market regime rather than fixed time intervals. Rebalancing is fundamentally a trade-off: more frequent adjustments reduce P&L swings and can amplify upside participation, but incur higher transaction costs and require active management discipline.
