# Stock Replacement

Stock replacement refers to using long-dated options, particularly LEAPS, as a leveraged alternative to owning shares outright, allowing traders to control a larger notional position with less capital. The strategy requires careful management of the Greeks—especially gamma, vega, and theta—across different expiration cycles to align the position with directional conviction and expected move magnitude while monitoring liquidity and bid-ask spreads. Stock replacement serves as an accessible entry point for traders transitioning from equities to options, offering a way to build complexity gradually while maintaining exposure to underlying price movements.
