# Research Methodology

Research methodology in options trading refers to the systematic, disciplined process of identifying, validating, and testing trading edges through rigorous statistical evaluation and hypothesis-driven analysis rather than relying on isolated signals or surface-level performance metrics. Effective research methodology requires integrating multiple data sources and analytical frameworks—such as COT reports, options chain analysis, and backtesting—while avoiding common pitfalls like outcome-focused bias and inadequate statistical rigor. Professional traders distinguish themselves by embedding research methodology as a core iterative process within their broader trading framework, using feedback loops and process-oriented learning to continuously refine strategy development and signal generation. Modern research methodology increasingly leverages AI-assisted tools and automated research terminals to scale hypothesis testing and market observation without requiring programming expertise.
