# Loss Management

Loss management is the predetermined plan for exiting or adjusting a trade when it moves against the trader, grounded in the underlying profit mechanism rather than arbitrary percentage targets. Effective loss management requires defining a maximum acceptable loss level before entering a trade—such as a 25% loss threshold in zero-DTE strategies—and committing to that rule through disciplined execution rather than emotional decision-making. The practice is essential because unmanaged losing positions systematically erode returns, while defined loss protocols enable traders to preserve capital and recover from drawdowns more effectively than passive holding or reactive liquidation.
