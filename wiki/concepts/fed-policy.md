# Fed Policy

Fed Policy refers to the Federal Reserve's decisions on interest rates and monetary tightening, which directly influence equity valuations, volatility expectations, and options pricing across markets. Traders monitor Fed rate-hike probabilities, pivot signals, and economic data (jobless claims, inflation readings) that shift rate expectations, as these shifts trigger capital reallocation between asset classes and can spark sharp moves in indices, individual equities, and volatility structures. Options traders use Fed policy shifts to anticipate market rallies or selloffs—such as expecting equity capitulation rallies when the Fed signals a pause or pivot, or positioning for weakness when rate-hike odds rise unexpectedly—and adjust strategies like covered strangles and earnings plays accordingly based on the macro regime.
