# Dividend

In options trading, dividends are a key driver of early assignment risk on short calls, particularly when a call moves deep in-the-money and the upcoming dividend payment exceeds the remaining extrinsic value, making early exercise economically rational for the call buyer. Traders managing covered calls or covered strangles must monitor positions ahead of dividend dates and can mitigate assignment risk through active rolling or position adjustments to preserve their underlying equity holdings. Dividend yield also factors into instrument selection, as lower-dividend ETFs like IWM offer better capital efficiency for income strategies compared to higher-yielding alternatives, while dividend stocks themselves can present yield-trap risks for retail traders who over-hedge or chase high yields without proper position management.
