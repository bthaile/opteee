# Win Rate

Win rate—the percentage of trades that close profitably—is a commonly misunderstood metric in options trading because high win rates do not guarantee profitability and can mask negative expected value and catastrophic tail risk. A strategy may achieve win rates of 80–97% while still losing money overall due to large losses on the remaining trades outweighing numerous small wins, a dynamic particularly evident in credit spreads where wide bid-ask fills and tail events can eliminate years of gains. Profitability depends on the relationship between win rate, average win size, average loss size, and position sizing rather than win rate alone, making it essential to validate edge through research and stress testing rather than relying on win-rate metrics to assess strategy viability.
