# Discipline

Discipline in options trading refers to the systematic adherence to a predetermined trading plan and process rather than chasing outcomes or reacting emotionally to market moves. It encompasses position sizing restraint, mechanical adjustment protocols, ruthless accountability through trade tracking, and the ability to execute consistently over years despite losses and unfavorable conditions. The channel emphasizes that discipline—not market knowledge or complex strategies—is the primary differentiator between successful traders and the majority who quit within five years, as it enables traders to eliminate ego, maintain process integrity, and benefit from edges that require patience and long holding periods that most market participants cannot sustain.
