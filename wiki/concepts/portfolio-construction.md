# Portfolio Construction

Portfolio construction in options trading involves assembling multiple positions within a defined capital base—such as a $25k account—using process-based decision-making that balances technical analysis, macro themes, and risk-adjusted position sizing rather than relying on directional conviction alone. Effective portfolio construction requires tracking individual trades and themes over time, matching signals to specific profit mechanisms, and managing drawdowns through disciplined entry and exit rules. Rather than pursuing hedging strategies that impose persistent drag (such as perpetual long puts), successful options portfolios often employ scenario-based or dynamic approaches that scale exposure based on market conditions while maintaining realistic position sizing given liquidity constraints.
