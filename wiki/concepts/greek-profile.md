# Greek Profile

A Greek profile is the combined exposure to delta, gamma, theta, and vega that a specific options structure carries at a given moment, determining how the position will behave across different market scenarios (directional moves, time decay, volatility shifts). Traders use Greek profiles to match their directional thesis and market edge to the right structure, since the same directional idea can be expressed through multiple positions with vastly different Greeks and P&L curves. Understanding Greek profile drift—how the Greeks change as market conditions evolve—is essential for knowing when a position requires adjustment to maintain alignment with the original trade thesis. The Greek profile becomes increasingly amplified and volatile in shorter-dated options (particularly 0DTE), requiring traders to account for speed, decay, and tail risk when selecting their expression.
