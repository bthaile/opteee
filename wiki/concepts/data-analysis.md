# Data Analysis

Data analysis in options trading involves systematically collecting, organizing, and examining trading data to identify edges, test strategies, and improve decision-making. This can range from maintaining detailed trading logs to track win rates and expectancy, to building comprehensive data warehouses with market history and research infrastructure that enable hypothesis-driven backtesting and strategy development. Traders use data analysis both to validate trading theses before risking capital—through paper trading and simulation—and to discover new patterns and signals that inform live trading. Modern approaches leverage AI tools and no-code frameworks to conduct this analysis without requiring programming expertise, allowing individual traders to process large datasets and generate actionable research at scale.
