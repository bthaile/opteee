# In The Money

In The Money (ITM) refers to an option position where the underlying asset's price has moved favorably relative to the strike price—a call is ITM when the underlying trades above the strike, and a put is ITM when it trades below. ITM options possess intrinsic value and exhibit higher delta, meaning they move more closely with the underlying asset, but they also carry reduced extrinsic value and face accelerated time decay as expiration approaches. Understanding moneyness is essential for trade construction because identical market moves produce vastly different profit and loss outcomes depending on whether positions are in, at, or out of the money, and deep ITM positions face risks like early assignment when extrinsic value diminishes.
