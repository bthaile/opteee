# Relative Value

Relative value is a trading strategy that identifies pricing discrepancies between related assets or markets by comparing their implied probabilities and valuations, then constructs trades to exploit those mispricings. Rather than making directional bets on individual assets, relative-value traders systematically measure how different profit mechanisms behave across correlated instruments and use structures like vertical spreads to isolate specific exposures while hedging others. This approach is one of several uncorrelated strategies—alongside carry and momentum—that retail traders can build to achieve sustainable returns without competing directly with institutional resources. The strategy requires understanding the shape, horizon, and robustness of the underlying market effect before selecting option structures, avoiding the fragility that comes from overfitting to single best-case scenarios.
