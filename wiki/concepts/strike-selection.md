# Strike Selection

Strike selection is the practice of choosing which price level to buy or sell an option at, and it fundamentally determines whether a directional thesis produces outsized gains, modest returns, or total losses even when the underlying moves as predicted. Rather than selecting strikes based on price targets, effective strike selection aligns the Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega) with the trader's thesis duration, conviction level, and market regime—matching how fast the move will occur, how volatility will behave, and how much time decay will erode the position. The same directional move can yield vastly different outcomes across four structurally different strikes depending on the interplay between speed, implied volatility, time-to-expiration, and leverage, making strike selection a core determinant of options trade construction and risk management.
