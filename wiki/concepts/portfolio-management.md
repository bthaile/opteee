# Portfolio Management

Portfolio management in options trading is the practice of coordinating risk and position sizing across three nested levels—portfolio, strategy, and trade—to ensure that individual edges generate consistent returns without catastrophic drawdowns. Rather than treating each trade in isolation, professional portfolio managers dynamically allocate capital, time put hedges to specific risk scenarios rather than carrying perpetual protection, and scale positions up or down based on market conditions and profit-locking opportunities. The discipline integrates profit mechanisms (the specific market effects being exploited), risk controls, and execution into a coherent system where position sizing becomes the primary lever for controlling outcomes, allowing the same trading edge to produce vastly different results depending on how capital is deployed across the portfolio structure.
