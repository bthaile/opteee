# Collar

A collar is an options strategy that combines a long put (downside protection) with a short call (upside cap) to create a defined-risk position with a capped payoff profile. The strategy finances the cost of downside protection by selling call premium, intentionally sacrificing upside potential to reduce losses during severe drawdowns and recover faster after systemic shocks. Traders use collars to balance risk reduction against opportunity cost, requiring active management as the underlying price moves and conviction evolves, since the position's path-dependent behavior between entry and expiration may diverge from the initial thesis.
