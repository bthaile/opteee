# Probability Of Profit

Probability of Profit (POP) is the statistical likelihood that an options position will be profitable at expiration, determined by strike selection, implied volatility, and time-to-expiration rather than directional conviction alone. Buying premium (long options) offers defined risk but lower probability of profit and requires larger directional moves, while selling premium (short options) offers higher probability of profit but undefined risk and requires active management. The critical insight is that high win rates do not guarantee profitability—position sizing and expected value matter more than raw probability—and that identical market moves can produce vastly different outcomes depending on the Greeks profile of the chosen strikes.
