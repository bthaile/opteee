# Valuation

Valuation in options trading refers to assessing whether an underlying asset's market price reflects its fundamental worth or intrinsic value, which directly influences option pricing and trading decisions. The concept appears across multiple contexts: evaluating whether an IPO or equity is richly or cheaply priced relative to earnings, sales multiples, or real returns (as with TIPS); determining if options are overpriced or underpriced relative to the underlying's true risk profile; and using valuation mismatches to identify trading opportunities, such as selling expensive calls or positioning around assets with questionable fundamental support. Traders use valuation analysis alongside options market structure—implied volatility, skew, and premium flows—to distinguish between narrative-driven price moves and those grounded in actual fundamental shifts.
