# Portfolio Margin

Portfolio margin is a capital-efficiency mechanism that allows traders to reduce upfront capital requirements for short options positions by posting margin based on portfolio risk rather than notional exposure. It enables strategies like naked short puts and large short straddles to be executed with substantially lower capital than cash-secured approaches, making it particularly valuable for scaling accounts and managing multiple positions simultaneously. The concept appears primarily in discussions of why vertical spreads are popular (as an alternative to portfolio-margin-backed naked shorts) and in live trade examples like short straddles on SPX, where portfolio margin enables traders to deploy significant premium-selling strategies with constrained capital.
