# Premium Selling

Premium selling is the practice of selling options contracts to collect the upfront payment (premium) paid by buyers, with the goal of profiting from time decay and volatility compression as expiration approaches. While low volatility environments do not eliminate premium income, premium sellers face asymmetric risk—frequent small gains interrupted by rare, large losses—requiring disciplined position sizing, delta hedging, and entry criteria to manage drawdowns effectively. Common premium-selling strategies include covered calls, cash-secured puts, and strangles, though backtesting shows that unmodified approaches often underperform buy-and-hold benchmarks due to capped upside, making strategic adjustments such as partial coverage, selective entry timing, and shorter time-to-expiration critical to improving risk-adjusted returns.
