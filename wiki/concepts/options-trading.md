# Options Trading

Options trading involves buying and selling contracts that grant the right to buy or sell an underlying asset at a specified price, used by traders to gain leveraged exposure, manage risk through strategies like covered strangles and married puts, and generate returns through both directional speculation and volatility positioning. Traders employ various approaches—from long-dated out-of-the-money calls as leveraged vehicles to deep in-the-money options held near expiration—and manage positions through time-based or price-target-based exits depending on their methodology and conviction. The strategy can be applied across different market regimes and asset classes, with performance driven by factors including skill maturation, favorable conditions, proper edge validation, and disciplined trade selection.
