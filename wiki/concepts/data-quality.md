# Data Quality

Data quality refers to the reliability, completeness, and accuracy of market data used to develop and validate trading strategies, and it directly impacts whether backtests and research produce meaningful insights or misleading results. Traders must ensure their data sources are properly integrated, free from gaps or errors, and stress-tested across different market regimes and historical periods to avoid building strategies that appear profitable in backtest but fail in live trading. The use of multiple data vendors, careful validation of signal logic, and rigorous analysis of friction costs (such as rebalancing expenses) are essential practices for maintaining data integrity throughout the research process.
